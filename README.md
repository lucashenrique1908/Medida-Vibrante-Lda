Medida Vibrante Lda

A responsive website developed for Medida Vibrante Lda, an air conditioning and climate solutions company based in Lisbon, Portugal.

The website was designed to present the company's services, projects and offers while providing customers with a simple way to request a quote and get in touch.

CSS3,
JavaScript,
Responsive Design,
Features,

Services and project showcase,
Promotional offers,
Customer reviews,
Quote request form,
WhatsApp integration,
Service area section,
Mobile-friendly navigation

Live Website: https://medidavibrante.com/

Developed for a real local business, with a focus on creating a professional online presence and a straightforward experience for potential customers.
